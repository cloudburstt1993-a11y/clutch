Acceptable use

Clutch shapes the input from your own controller. How you use it is up to you, and so is the responsibility.

  • Do not use Clutch to break the rules of a game. Many online games treat automated or scripted inputs as a violation and ban accounts for it. Clutch's advanced mods exist for offline play. Using them in an online game is against these terms.
  • Online-safe mode is on by default and pauses ban-risk mods in online games. You may turn it off (with the parent PIN on Pro and Family), but if you do, what happens to a game account is on you. No tool can promise zero risk, and we do not promise it.
  • Do not use Clutch in any tournament, league or competition that prohibits input modification.
  • Do not use Clutch to harass, harm or deceive other players.
  • In the forum, follow the Community Guidelines. We may remove content, mute or ban accounts that do not.
