The 14-day policy

If Clutch Pro or Family is not for you, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. You do not need to give a reason, though telling us helps us make Clutch better.

  • Applies to Clutch Pro and Clutch Family.
  • Counted from the date on your receipt.
  • Refunded to the original payment method by our merchant of record.

How to ask

  1. Go to support and pick the topic License or purchase.
  2. Include the email address you bought with and, if you have it, the order number from your receipt.
  3. We confirm the refund by email. Refunds usually show on your statement within a few business days, depending on your bank. [Confirm typical timing with the merchant of record.]

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to send purchase or refund questions.

What happens to your license

When a refund is issued, the license key is deactivated. Clutch on your PC drops back to the Free plan: remaps, stick and trigger tuning, and the starter game packs keep working. Your profiles and settings stay on your PC, so if you buy again later, you pick up where you left off.

Family plan

Family (3 PC activations) has the same 14-day terms as Pro. A refund deactivates the key on all PCs it was used on. We do not offer partial refunds for unused activations.
