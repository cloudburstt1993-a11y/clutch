What the app does on your PC

Clutch runs on your Windows PC. Your profiles, power-up settings, game packs, parent PIN, quiet hours, crosshair choices and Aim Lab practice scores are stored locally on that PC. They are not uploaded to us.

Clutch reads your controller's input in order to shape it and pass it to the game through a virtual controller. It does not record what you type or do in other programs.

Website and forum data

If you create a community account, we store:

  • Your username and the pixel avatar you picked.
  • Your email address, used for sign-in, password resets and nothing else unless you ask.
  • Your posts, replies, upvotes and reports, and the badges you have earned.
  • The age confirmation you gave at sign-up (that you are 13 or older), not your birth date.

Posts are public. Anyone can read them without an account. There are no private messages on the forum, on purpose.

If you send a support message, we keep what you sent (first name or username, email, topic, controller, message) for as long as we need it to help you. [Confirm where support submissions are stored and the retention period.]

Children under 13 (COPPA)

The Clutch community is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly create accounts for, or collect personal information from, anyone under 13. We ask for age confirmation at sign-up and do not use the answer for anything else.

If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete the account and the data that goes with it. If you are a parent and believe your child under 13 has an account, contact support and we will remove it.

Kids under 13 can still use the Clutch app on a PC with a parent or guardian's purchase, because the app itself does not create accounts or collect personal information from them. [Confirm with developer that the app collects nothing that could identify a child.]

What's never shown

By default, the community shows usernames only. There is no place to enter a real name, upload a photo, or set a location, and we do not display any of those. Avatars come from a fixed set of pixel art.

Our Community Guidelines also ask members not to post personal information about themselves or anyone else, including names, schools, cities, photos and social media handles. Moderators remove it when they see it, and every post has a report button.

Payments

Cookies and localStorage

This website uses your browser's localStorage to remember things like whether you are logged in, the waitlist you joined, and demo data while the site is in test mode. These values stay in your browser and are not sent to us by the website itself. Clearing your browser storage removes them.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. [Confirm with developer: list any analytics tool, if one is added, and how to opt out.] The merchant of record and the Lemon Squeezy checkout overlay, if enabled, may set their own cookies during checkout.
