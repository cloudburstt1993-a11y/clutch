What Clutch is

Clutch is a Windows PC app that upgrades any game controller: Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, GameSir and more. Your kid taps a power-up (like hair triggers or smooth aim), picks a ready-made game pack, and plays. There are no scripts and no coding. It runs on the PC, it is free to start, and the paid version is a one-time purchase with no subscription.

  • Windows 10 and 11
  • Works with the controller you already own
  • Online-safe mode is on the first time it opens
  • Parent controls are included with Pro and Family

Online-safe mode ON BY DEFAULT

Some controller mods are fine in single-player games but can get an account flagged in online games. Online-safe mode pauses those risky mods whenever your kid is playing online, and it is switched on from the first launch.

Kids keep the feel they like for offline play. In online games, the risky mods simply stay off. Turning Online-safe mode off is a parent decision and sits behind the parent PIN.

Honest note: no tool can promise zero risk. Online-safe mode is designed to keep accounts in good standing, and it is the default, but every game publisher sets its own rules.

Quiet hours & break reminders PRO

Quiet hours are the times Clutch is off. Pick a window, like school nights after 9 pm, and the app's power-ups and packs pause until it ends. Break reminders put a gentle "take five" on screen on a schedule you choose.

Both live behind the parent PIN, so the schedule you set is the schedule that sticks.

A profile for every kid

Each kid gets their own profile with their own packs, settings and practice progress. Nobody overwrites anybody else's setup. Profiles are picked from inside the app, so there is no login for kids to manage.

Family covers 3 PCs with one purchase, so siblings on different machines each get their own space.

Parent FAQ

Will my kid get banned?

Online-safe mode pauses ban-risk mods in online games, and it is on by default. That is the whole point of it: the mods that could get an account flagged stay off whenever your kid is playing online.

That said, no tool can promise zero risk. Every game publisher sets and enforces its own rules, and those rules change. Clutch is built to keep accounts in good standing, and the parent PIN keeps Online-safe mode where you set it.

Is there a subscription?

No. Clutch Free is free. Pro is $19.99 one time. Family is $29.99 one time and covers 3 PCs. Pay once and it is yours. There is nothing to cancel.

Which controllers work?

Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, GameSir and more. If it connects to a Windows PC by USB or Bluetooth as a game controller, Clutch can work with it. The guides cover setup for each type.

Do I need to know coding?

No. Neither do your kids. There are no scripts to write and no files to edit. Every power-up is a single tap, and game packs are ready-made. If something feels off, the "How does it feel?" question tunes it for you.

How do I buy it for my kid?

Checkout is a parent or guardian purchase, and buyers must be 18 or older. You pay once, your license key is emailed to you and shown in your account, and your kid enters the key in Clutch under Settings → License. See pricing.

What is the refund policy?

There is a 14-day refund policy on Pro and Family. Full details are on the Refund Policy page.

Can my kid talk to strangers in the forum?

Only in public. The community is 13+ with an age gate at sign-up. There are no private messages, so every post is visible to everyone and to moderators. Members use usernames and preset pixel avatars, not real names or photos. Every post has a report button, there is a profanity filter, and a moderator queue reviews reports. The community rules are short and kid-friendly: be kind, no sharing personal info, no selling or trading.
