NEW IN 13.1

Two ways to play on console right now

No box, no waiting. Clutch 13.1 brings your tuning to the console you already own — streamed in over official Remote Play, or matched in the game's own settings.

REMOTE PLAY MODE

Your mods, streamed in

Run Clutch on your PC and stream your Xbox to it with the official Xbox app. Your tuned controller rides that connection to the console, so curves, gyro, crosshair and macros all work. Console mode re-tunes the sticks for the stream's small delay.

SETTINGS ADVISOR

Match it on the console

Playing straight on the console instead? Clutch turns your profile into the exact deadzone, response-curve and sensitivity values to enter in the game's own menu — pick your game and copy them across.

NO HARDWARE

Nothing to buy or plug in

Both work with the Xbox and controller you already have. Nothing on the console is modified — the Xbox app does the talking.
