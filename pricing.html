FAQ

Pricing questions

What happens after I pay?

Your license key is emailed to you and appears in your account. Install Clutch from the download page, open Settings → License, and paste the key. Pro features switch on right away.

Can I move my license to a new PC?

Yes. On the old PC, open Settings → License and choose Deactivate. Then enter your key on the new PC. Family keys have 3 activations, so you can also just add the new PC if you have one free.

Can I upgrade from Free to Pro, or Pro to Family?

Free to Pro or Family: just buy the plan you want and enter the key. Pro to Family: contact support and we will sort it out with you.

Is there really no subscription?

Really. Pro is $19.99 once. Family is $29.99 once. There is nothing to cancel and nothing that renews.

Can I buy the Console Kit now?

Not yet. The Console Kit is coming soon, from $79 with Pro included. You can join the waitlist; no payment is taken and no date is promised.
