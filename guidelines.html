The rules

The Clutch forum is a place to share setups, fix problems and vote on what gets built next. It works because everyone follows a few simple rules. They are short on purpose.

1. Be kind

  • Treat people the way you want to be treated.
  • New players ask basic questions. Answer them like someone once answered yours.
  • No insults, no name-calling, no making fun of anyone.

2. No cheating talk in online games

  • Do not ask for, share or brag about ways to break the rules of an online game.
  • Help with setup, power-ups, packs and offline play is always welcome.
  • Posts about getting around a game's rules get removed, and repeat posts get a ban.

3. No sharing personal info

  • Not yours, not anyone else's.
  • That means real names, school, city, photos, phone numbers, email addresses and social media accounts.
  • Your username and pixel avatar are all anyone needs to know.

4. No selling or trading

  • No selling, buying or trading anything: accounts, keys, controllers, in-game items, anything.
  • No links to stores or marketplaces.
  • Sharing a free profile or game pack you made is great. That is not selling.

5. Keep it public

  • There are no private messages here, on purpose. Everything is out in the open.
  • Do not ask people to move the conversation somewhere private.
  • If you want to help someone, reply in the thread so everyone learns.

6. Report, don't fight

  • See something that breaks the rules? Tap the report button on the post. Every post has one.
  • Do not argue with the person or pile on. Moderators handle it.
  • Reports are private. Nobody sees who reported.

What moderators do

Moderators are here to keep the forum friendly. When a post is reported, it goes into a queue and a moderator looks at it. Depending on what happened, they can:

  • Remove the post and let you know why.
  • Mute an account for a while, so it can read but not post.
  • Ban an account that keeps breaking the rules or does something serious.

Moderators try to be fair and explain their decisions. If you think a decision was wrong, you can ask about it through support. A profanity filter also catches the obvious stuff automatically.

Badges you can earn

Badges show up next to your username. They are earned, not bought (except Pro, which just means you have a Pro license).

EARLY SUPPORTER PRO BETA TESTER HELPER
  • Early supporter: you were here in the first days.
  • Pro: you have a Clutch Pro or Family license.
  • Beta tester: you helped test something before it shipped, like the Console Kit.
  • Helper: your answers keep getting marked as the fix. The best badge there is.

The founder has a DEV badge, and dev posts are highlighted so you can spot them.

13+ only

You have to be 13 or older to have a forum account. We ask when you sign up. If you are under 13, you can still use the Clutch app with a parent's help, and you can read the forum without an account. If we find an account that belongs to someone under 13, we remove it and its data. See the Privacy Policy.

For parents

The forum is built to be a safe place for teens to talk about controllers and games. There are no private messages, no real names, no photos and no locations. Every post has a report button, reports go to a moderator queue, and a profanity filter runs on everything. Accounts are usernames only, and we do not create accounts for children under 13. If you ever want a post or an account looked at, contact support and a person will reply.
