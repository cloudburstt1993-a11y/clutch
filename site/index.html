New in 13.1

Your mods, on your console.

Run Clutch on your PC and stream your Xbox to it with Microsoft's own Xbox app. Your tuned controller rides that official connection to the console — so your curves, gyro, crosshair and macros all work while you play.

No adapters, no extra hardware, and nothing on the console is modified. Flip on Console mode and Clutch re-tunes the sticks for the small delay a stream adds, so aim stays predictable.

  • Works over official Xbox Remote Play
  • Playing straight on console? The Settings Advisor hands you the exact in-game values to match your profile
  • No controller authentication touched — the Xbox app does the talking
  1. Turn on remote features

    On your Xbox: Settings → Devices & connections → Remote features.

  2. Open the Xbox app on PC

    Free from the Microsoft Store. Sign in and connect to your console.

  3. Keep Clutch running

    Its virtual controller is what streams to the console, so your profile rides along.

  4. Flip Console mode on

    Clutch tunes for the stream and you play — mods and all.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Will Clutch get me banned? +
Clutch uses standard controller input your system already trusts — no injection, no game-file edits. Turn on Online-safe mode and it disables anything a competitive anti-cheat might flag. Every game's rules are its own, so the final call on what you run online is yours.
Does it really work on Xbox? +
Yes, over Remote Play. Clutch runs on your PC, the Xbox app streams your console to it, and your tuned controller rides that official connection to the console. Playing straight on the console instead? The Settings Advisor gives you the exact in-game values to match your profile.
What do I need to run it? +
Windows 10 or 11 and just about any controller — Xbox, PlayStation, or a generic PC pad. It also runs on Windows handhelds. No extra hardware or adapters required.
One-time or subscription? +
One-time. You buy the version you want and it's yours — no recurring charge. Pro includes a 14-day trial up front.
