Hair triggers

Fire the instant you touch the trigger instead of pulling it all the way down.

Why it helpsLess travel means your shot or brake happens the moment you decide, not a split second later.

SCREENSHOT: smooth-aim-toggle
FREE

Smooth aim

Takes the jitter out of your right stick so small movements stay small.

Why it helpsYour crosshair goes where you point it instead of twitching past the target.

SCREENSHOT: steady-aim-toggle
FREE

Steady aim

Keeps your crosshair from drifting when you let go of the stick.

Why it helpsWorn sticks drift. Steady aim stops the slow slide so you stay lined up when you stop moving.
